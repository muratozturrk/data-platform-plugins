# Sources

## Books
- Stephen Few. *Information Dashboard Design: Displaying Data for At-a-Glance Monitoring*, 2nd ed. Analytics Press, 2013. Definition of a dashboard, thirteen common mistakes, visual perception applied to dashboards, bullet graphs, sparklines.
- Stephen Few. *Show Me the Numbers: Designing Tables and Graphs to Enlighten*, 2nd ed. Analytics Press, 2012. Table/graph selection and design.
- Stephen Few. *Now You See It* (2009) and *Signal* (2015). Analytical dashboards and detecting meaningful change.
- Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic. *Storytelling with Data: A Data Visualization Guide for Business Professionals*. Wiley, 2015. Context, choosing displays, decluttering, focusing attention, narrative.
- Edward R. Tufte. *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*, 2nd ed. Graphics Press, 2001. Data-ink ratio, chart junk, small multiples.
- Edward R. Tufte. *Envisioning Information* (1990), *Beautiful Evidence* (2006).
- Alberto Cairo. *The Functional Art* (2012), *The Truthful Art* (2016). Perception, honesty in charts.
- Dona M. Wong. *The Wall Street Journal Guide to Information Graphics*. Norton, 2010. Chart choice, colour, formatting rules.
- Jacques Bertin. *Semiology of Graphics* (1967/2010). Visual variables.
- Andy Kirk. *Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design*, 2nd ed. Sage, 2019.
- Steve Wexler, Jeffrey Shaffer, Andy Cotgreave. *The Big Book of Dashboards*. Wiley, 2017. 28 real-world dashboards with critiques.
- Brent Dykes. *Effective Data Storytelling*. Wiley, 2019.

## Research and guides
- Cleveland & McGill, "Graphical Perception" (1984): accuracy ranking of visual encodings (position > length > angle > area > colour).
- Financial Times Visual Vocabulary; Datawrapper "Which chart" guides; ColorBrewer / Okabe-Ito colour-blind-safe palettes; WCAG 2.2 contrast.
- Superset docs: Creating charts and dashboards, Native filters, Cross-filtering, Drill to detail/drill by, Theming (6.x), Matrixify (6.1).
- Preset blog: dashboard best-practice posts and 6.0 theming deep dive.
