# Sources

## Books (rules paraphrased, chapters cited)
- Nicholas Desbarats, *Practical Charts* (2023): ch 1 non-data elements, ch 2 scales, ch 3 colour, ch 4 legends, ch 5 formatting numbers/dates/text, ch 6 time, ch 7 part-to-whole, ch 8 maps, ch 9 bar charts, ch 10 tables, ch 11 combo charts, ch 12 chart types to avoid.
- Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, *Storytelling with Data* (2015): ch 2 choosing an effective visual, ch 3 clutter.
- Andy Kirk, *Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design*, 2nd ed. (2019): ch 6 data representation.
- Stephen Few, *Information Dashboard Design* (2006): ch 4 visual perception, ch 6 display media.
