# Sources

- Camille Fournier, Ian Nowland. *Platform Engineering: A Guide for Technical, Product, and People Leaders*. O'Reilly, 2024. Part II covers product mindset, platform product management, operating and scaling platforms.
- CNCF TAG App Delivery, Platforms Working Group. *Platforms White Paper* (2023) and *Platform Engineering Maturity Model* (v1, Oct 2023). https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platform-eng-maturity-model/
- Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais. *Team Topologies*. IT Revolution, 2019. Platform teams, Thinnest Viable Platform, cognitive load. https://teamtopologies.com/key-concepts-content/what-is-a-thinnest-viable-platform-tvp
- Zhamak Dehghani. *Data Mesh: Delivering Data-Driven Value at Scale*. O'Reilly, 2022. Self-serve data platform, multiplane platform, data product as architecture quantum.
- Spotify / Backstage. Golden paths and software templates. https://backstage.io
- Databricks, "Lessons from building large-scale, multi-cloud SaaS software" (Data+AI Summit): the factory that builds and evolves the platform matters more than the platform itself; product analytics on the platform's own usage.
