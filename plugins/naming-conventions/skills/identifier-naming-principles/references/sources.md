# Sources

## Empirical research
- Dror G. Feitelson, Ayelet Mizrahi, Nofar Noy, Aviad Ben Shabat, Or Eliyahu, Roy Sheffer. "How Developers Choose Names." *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering* 48(1), 2022, pp. 37–52. doi:10.1109/TSE.2020.2976920 (arXiv:2103.07487). 334 subjects; median 6.9% name agreement; three-step naming model.
- Rachel Alpern, Ido Lazer, Issar Tzachor, Hanit Hakim, Sapir Weissbuch, Dror G. Feitelson. "Reproducing, Extending, and Analyzing Naming Experiments." arXiv:2402.10022, 2024. Replicates the model; explicitly following it yields better names, "just use longer names" does not.
- Simon Butler, Michel Wermelinger, Yijun Yu, Helen Sharp. "Relating Identifier Naming Flaws and Code Quality: An Empirical Study." WCRE 2009. 12 guidelines; flawed identifiers associated with static-analysis issues.
- Venera Arnaoudova, Massimiliano Di Penta, Giuliano Antoniol. "Linguistic Antipatterns: What They Are and How Developers Perceive Them." *Empirical Software Engineering*, 2016.
- Eran Avidan, Dror G. Feitelson. "Effects of Variable Names on Comprehension: An Empirical Study." ICPC 2017.
- Dawn Lawrie, Christopher Morrell, Henry Feild, David Binkley. "What's in a Name? A Study of Identifiers." ICPC 2006 (full words > abbreviations > single letters for comprehension).

## Books

- Steve McConnell. *Code Complete*, 2nd ed. Microsoft Press, 2004. Ch. 11 "The Power of Variable Names".
- Robert C. Martin. *Clean Code*. Prentice Hall, 2008. Ch. 2 "Meaningful Names".
- John Ousterhout. *A Philosophy of Software Design*, 2nd ed. 2021. Ch. 14 "Choosing Names".
- Kevlin Henney (ed.). *97 Things Every Programmer Should Know*. O'Reilly, 2010 (several naming essays).
